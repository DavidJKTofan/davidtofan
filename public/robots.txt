# Legal pages (/ai-licensing-terms/, /disclaimer/, /imprint/) are deliberately NOT
# disallowed here. They are served with `noindex, nofollow` (meta tag + X-Robots-Tag)

# fetching the page, so the noindex would never be read and the URL could still be
# indexed without a description. Crawlable + noindex is what actually de-indexes.
# Do not add Disallow rules for them.
